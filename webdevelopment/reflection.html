Personal Reflection

Introduction

I have always been interested in computers since I was young. My curiosity about how technology works led me to pursue a degree in Information Technology and build a career in the field. Over the years, I have learned to adapt to different specializations based on the needs of the companies I have worked with. This flexibility has helped me grow and gain valuable experience in various areas of IT.


Career Goals

I plan to continue working in IT, focusing on automation of IT processes, data engineering, and similar fields. As I grow in my career, I also want to help others who are interested in IT by providing guidance and support. When I first started, I didn’t have much direction, and I know how difficult that can be. I promised myself that I would do my best to help others navigate the challenges I faced and prepare them for their own journey.


Teaching Philosophy

I believe the best way to learn is through hands-on experience. While studying theory is important, nothing compares to actually working on real-world problems and finding solutions. Hands-on learning allows people to develop practical skills, build confidence, and understand concepts more deeply. In IT, technology is constantly changing, and learning by doing helps students stay adaptable and ready for new challenges. Whether it's coding, troubleshooting, or creating new solutions, engaging directly with the work is the most effective way to learn.


Conclusion

I hope to continue growing in the field of IT, mentoring others along the way, and making a positive impact on society. Technology has the power to improve lives, and I want to contribute to meaningful advancements that help people and businesses succeed. Through my experiences, I have learned the value of hard work, continuous learning, and supporting others, and I plan to carry these principles forward in my career.
